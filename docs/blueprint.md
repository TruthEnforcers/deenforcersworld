# **App Name**: Truth Enforcers Bible Church Int'l (TEBCI)

## Core Features:

- Homepage & Navigation: A dynamic homepage featuring a responsive header/navigation bar with the church logo and 'TEBCI' name, prominent navigation links for large screens, and a 'bouncing arrow' navigation indicator for small screens. The landing page welcomes visitors with an inspiring image and text: 'Welcome to **Truth Enforcers Bible Church Intl** AKA: Messengers Of Truth. Welcome To De Enforcers World. Here! We live and overcome by the truth.' The banner includes call-to-action buttons for 'About US', 'Live stream', and 'Give Online'. Subsequent sections include a 'Quote of the Month', a dedicated 'Mandate and About' section, a highlights section using cards to navigate to 'About Us', 'Upcoming Programs', and 'Give Offerings'. There's an 'Upcoming Events' section with a monthly events carousel, a section introducing the Lead Pastor with a short bio and picture, a section with cards to 'Listen to Messages', a 'Ministries' section with individual ministry cards, a 'Testimonies' section from members, and a 'See You In Church' section with service times against a captivating background, concluding with a comprehensive footer.
- Sermon Archive: Browse and stream past sermons in audio or video format, with categorization by date or topic, accessible via 'Listen to Messages' cards.
- Events Calendar: View an up-to-date calendar of upcoming church events, meetings, and community gatherings, integrated with the 'Upcoming Events' carousel.
- Contact Form: A simple contact form for visitors to submit inquiries, prayer requests, or general messages to the church administration.
- Announcements/News Feed: A dedicated section to display important church announcements, news, and updates for the congregation.
- Online Giving Integration: A clear and direct link to an external, secure platform for online donations and tithing, accessible via the 'Give Online' banner button and 'Give Offerings' card.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Muted deep indigo (#564766) representing wisdom and serenity. HSL: H(270) S(30%) L(40%).
- Background color: Very light, cool grey-purple (#F8F7FA) providing an airy and spacious feel. HSL: H(270) S(10%) L(97%).
- Accent color: Vibrant blue-violet (#5E46EA) to highlight calls to action and important information, ensuring strong contrast. HSL: H(240) S(70%) L(55%).
- Headline font: 'Literata' (serif) for a dignified and timeless aesthetic. Body font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for clear readability and a modern touch.
- Utilize simple, clean, and recognizable outline icons to maintain a minimalist yet functional appearance, enhancing navigation.
- Implement a clean, organized, and fully responsive layout that prioritizes readability and easy access to information across all devices, featuring a flexible header/nav bar, card-based content sections, and a carousel for events.
- Subtle and gentle transitions on navigation elements and content loading. A distinctive 'bouncing arrow' animation will indicate navigation on small screens.
